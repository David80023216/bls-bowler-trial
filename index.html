Import a BLS export

In BLS: Reports → Bowler Reports → Export (CSV/delimited — this export includes each bowler's high game and high series), or export the standing sheet as HTML, or paste standing-sheet text. Name the bowling center and league, import once, and every bowler at that center can find themselves. Re-import after league night to refresh everyone's stats.

Your stats are loaded automatically from your league's latest BLS upload — when your secretary's newest stats go in, yours update too. High game / high series show when the upload is the BLS Bowler Report export; a standing sheet carries averages, pins and games only, so those two show “—” until a Bowler Report is loaded.
